## 1. Project Overview

This project involves the design and development of a website
for Home Baked, a family-owned bakery located in Durban North.

The purpose of the website is to establish a professional online
presence for the business and make it easier for customers to
view products, find contact information and make enquiries.

Home Baked was selected because the business does not currently
have a dedicated website. This provides an opportunity to design
and develop a website from the ground up.

---

## 2. Organisation

### Home Baked

Home Baked is a local bakery offering freshly baked products
including cakes, cupcakes, bread, pastries and other baked goods.

The website is designed to provide customers with information
about the bakery, its products, contact details and enquiry options.

---

## 3. Website Goals

The main goals of the website are:

1. Establish a professional online presence for Home Baked.
2. Make product information easily accessible to customers.
3. Provide customers with an easy way to make enquiries.
4. Provide accurate contact and location information.
5. Improve the customer's overall experience when interacting
   with the business online.
